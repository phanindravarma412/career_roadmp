## Rules

1. **AI off for all practice.** Type everything; no copy-paste from old projects.
2. **Docs allowed until Week 4.** From Week 10, practise without docs and against a timer.
3. **Daily check-in with me** after each day; move on only if the average is 3.5+ and no topic is 2 or below (details in `daily-plan.md`).
4. **One practice repo** (`career-roadmap/`) on GitHub, committed daily. Your deployed full-stack app goes on your resume.

## Risks

- **Skipping DSA days.** This is the gap that decides offers. Protect the daily slot over everything else.
- **Practising with AI on.** It will feel productive and teach you nothing for the interview.
- **Rushing the foundations.** If an exit check fails, repeat the weak days before moving on.
- **Spreading too thin.** Full-stack + Flutter is wide. If you fall behind, cut Flutter days first, never DSA or backend.
- **60-day notice.** Some recruiters filter you out. Say "60 days, open to discussing an early release."
- **Holiday slowdown.** If Week 13 lands in the last two weeks of December, push main applications to early January.
- **Start date drift.** Pick a Monday, write it at the top, and block the daily 2 hours on your calendar.
